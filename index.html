The Company

Two decades of precision supply

Founded in 2006 and run by Ajay Nayak, Partsco India is a proprietorship trading, wholesaling and distributing spare parts for bar cutting and bending machinery from Kolkata, West Bengal.

Every product we list is a component we know intimately — the blades, pins, checking blocks, couplings, connecting rods, shafts, and electricals that keep rebar fabrication running. Buyers get real parts, real specs, and a direct line to the person who knows the machine.
